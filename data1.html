Task: Analyze a Small Dataset

Imagine you have the following data about the number of hours students studied in one week: 

      Alice: 5
      Bob: 8
      Carol: 2
      David: 10
      Emma: 7

Your Task:

  • Find the student who studied the most hours.
  • Find the student who studied the least hours.
  • Calculate the average study hours.

You can do this using pen & paper or with a simple Python code like this: 

      data = {"Alice": 5, "Bob": 8, "Carol": 2, "David": 10, "Emma": 7}
      print("Max:", max(data, key=data.get))
      print("Min:", min(data, key=data.get))
      print("Average:", sum(data.values()) / len(data))
