Sample English Questions

  1. Fill in the blank: I ____ to the park yesterday.
    • a) go
    • b) went
    • c) gone
  2. Which sentence is correct?
    • a) She don’t like apples.
    • b) She doesn’t likes apples.
    • c) She doesn’t like apples.
  3. Translate to English: "او هر روز کتاب می‌خواند."
    • a) He reads a book every day.
    • b) He read a book every day.
    • c) He is reading a book every day.

Answers: 1-b, 2-c, 3-a
