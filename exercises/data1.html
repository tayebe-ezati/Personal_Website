Data Science Exercise 1

In this exercise, you will work with a simple dataset.

Task

Imagine you have the following dataset of students and their scores:

Name Score
Ali 85
Sara 92
Reza 74

Your task:

  • Find the student with the highest score.
  • Find the student with the lowest score.
  • Calculate the average score.

You can do this manually or using Python (if you know the basics).
