Practice Today

Here are some exercises to improve your English. Try them and check your answers!

Vocabulary – Weather

Fill in the blanks:

  1. It’s very _______ today, don’t forget your umbrella. (rainy / sunny)
  2. Los Angeles often has _______ summers. (hot / cold)
  3. In winter, it sometimes _______. (snows / rains)

Speaking – Daily Conversation

Practice saying these sentences aloud:

  • “This is hard to watch.”
  • “If I were you, I would go to Madrid.”
  • “Could you please tell me where the nearest store is?”

Writing – Short Task

Write 3–4 sentences about the weather in your city today.
